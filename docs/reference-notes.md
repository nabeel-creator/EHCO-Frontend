# EHCO Reference Site Analysis

Comparative analysis of three relevant international and local NGO sites, focusing on information architecture, navigation behavior, and donation placement.

---

## 1. UNDP Careers (undp.org/careers)

- **Navigation pattern:** Multi-tiered global navigation with top utility bar for organizational taxonomy and clean, text-driven primary navigation.
- **Where Donate sits:** Located in the global utility header as a high-contrast accent button that persists across all sub-portals, ensuring high visibility without interfering with career search flows.
- **How listings are filtered:** Faceted sidebar filters (location, topic, experience level) coupled with instant URL query parameters for bookmarkable states.
- **Accessibility & mobile behavior:** Keyboard accessible disclosure menus with visible outline rings and clear escape triggers; collapses into an off-canvas drawer on mobile.
- **One thing NOT to copy:** Extremely dense multi-column mega-menus with multiple sub-levels of bureaucratic categorization that overwhelm mobile users and older audiences.

---

## 2. WWF Pakistan (wwfpak.org)

- **Navigation pattern:** Streamlined single-tier navigation categorized by core mission pillars (Our Work, Where We Work, How to Help, Publications).
- **Where Donate sits:** Sticky top-right position in bright contrast, accompanied by a secondary callout banner in the hero fold.
- **How listings are filtered:** Categorical dropdowns and tagged project grids, allowing donors and educators to browse by conservation biome or region.
- **Accessibility & mobile behavior:** Large touch targets on mobile with simple hamburger navigation, prioritizing contact and donation channels.
- **One thing NOT to copy:** Heavy auto-rotating hero image carousels that distract users, consume excessive bandwidth, and cause layout shifts on slow cellular connections.

---

## 3. Zero Waste Foundation COP Portal (cop.sifiratikvakfi.org/en/)

- **Navigation pattern:** Focused single-event narrative navigation combining jump links with dedicated summit pages and multilateral declarations.
- **Where Donate sits:** Positioned under an "Act With Us / Support" CTA block rather than an overt donation button, prioritizing institutional partnership and pledge commitments.
- **How listings are filtered:** Chronological event agenda and publication list with date-based segmented tabs.
- **Accessibility & mobile behavior:** High-contrast color blocks with clean typographic hierarchy, ensuring readability across varied screen sizes.
- **One thing NOT to copy:** Heavy decorative parallax scroll effects and complex interactive graphics that cause frame drops on mid-tier and older mobile devices.
